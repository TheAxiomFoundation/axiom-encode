An ASCII mixed fraction before a percent word is one rate ("16 1/2 אחוזים" is 0.165), a fraction noun before the percent word is a fraction of a percent ("חמישית אחוז" is 0.002), a mixed number takes a counted fractional tail ("אחד ושני שלישים" is five thirds), a structural reference accepts the whole numeral grammar ("סעיף מאה ועשרים" is a reference, not a 120 to recall), and the compound scan no longer copies the remaining run at each candidate word.
