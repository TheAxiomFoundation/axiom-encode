A bare vav pair shares a percent unit or a scale word only under "בהתאמה" after the unit, the marker of a paired list ("2 ו־3 אחוזים, בהתאמה", "1 ו־2 ו־3 מיליון שקלים, בהתאמה"); without it the vav joins clauses as often ("מספר העובדים הוא 50 ו־10% מהם זכאים" keeps 50, "ההכנסה היא 500 ו־2% ממנה" keeps 500), and a currency code before or after an operand ("USD 500", "500 EUR"), at any spacing, marks it an amount as a sign does.
