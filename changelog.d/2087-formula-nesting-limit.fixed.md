The formula parser now accepts at most 64 nested levels, so its answer depends only on the formula's text. A parenthesized group, a bracket index, a call's arguments and a unary operator each open a level; an empty call such as `f()` opens none. Before, a deeply nested formula could pass with stack to spare and fail with less, and the failure could stay in the branch cache. With too little stack to parse, the call now raises `RecursionError` and caches nothing.
