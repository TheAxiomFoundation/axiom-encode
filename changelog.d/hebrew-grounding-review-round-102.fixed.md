A list heading governs only the list body right after it, so a heading completed earlier in the clause leaves a later predicate alone ("שיעורי המס הם 10% ו־20%, הקנס יהיה 50 ו־2%" keeps the fine at 50), any connector joins within a headed list ("שיעורי המס הם 10, 20 או 30 אחוזים" lists three rates), and the colon that introduces a list ("השיעורים כדלקמן: 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים") no longer cuts the heading off.
