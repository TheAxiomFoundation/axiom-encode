Pair a list chapeau such as "if they are:" with the items that follow it. A case pair now satisfies the chapeau when one of those items names the toggled input in full, in an excerpt the toggling rule cites. 12 CFR 1006.14(b)(3) states its exclusions only in its items, so the complete-source check couldn't pair it before. Excerpts from elsewhere in the toggling rule don't count. A clause with a condition of its own, such as "if the household so elects, the deductions are:", still needs a pair on that condition.
