Numeric recall no longer demands a single subdivision locator that ends where a clause cue begins, such as `pursuant to this subdivision 7 if such payments` or `subdivision B 10 of § 58.1-301 related to loans` (#1831). The reference needs evidence of its own: `this` before the keyword, or a statutory object after the label. A unit word, a suffix letter, a coordinated label or a reference without that evidence keeps the number demanded, and so does the same number anywhere it is operative.

The `source-explicit-conditions` gate no longer splits a carryforward consequent into a second condition (#1832). In `If X exceeds $ 4,000, the remainder may be carried forward and subtracted in future taxable years until ... has been fully deducted`, the consequent's `and` joins effects, so the clause states one condition. Only a closed carryforward consequent is recognized, and only when cutting it off loses no antecedent gate; a condition, relative clause or factual `until` clause in the consequent keeps main's gates, and `if A and B, ...` still demands two terminals.
