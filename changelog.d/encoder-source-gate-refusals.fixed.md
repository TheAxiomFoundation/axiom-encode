Numeric recall no longer demands a single subdivision locator that ends where a clause cue begins, such as `subdivision B 10 of § 58.1-301 related to loans`, or `pursuant to this subdivision 7 if such payments` inside item `7.` (#1831). The reference needs evidence of its own: a statutory object after the label, or a self-reference to the enclosing item in a clause with its own verb. A bare `this subdivision N if` keeps the number, because in gapped English the number can be the elided value. A unit word, a suffix letter, a coordinated label or a reference without that evidence keeps the number demanded, and so does the same number anywhere it is operative.

The `source-explicit-conditions` gate no longer splits a carryforward consequent into a second condition (#1832). In `If X exceeds $ 4,000, the remainder may be carried forward and subtracted in future taxable years until ... has been fully deducted`, the consequent's `and` joins effects, so the clause states one condition. Only a closed carryforward consequent is recognized, and its exhausted amount must be plain words. The cut happens only when every antecedent segment keeps a gate of its own and the antecedent has no top-level comma, relative pronoun or condition cue. A condition the splitter cannot gate (`and the taxpayer elects`), a relative clause, a nested conditional, a factual `until` clause, or a restriction on whose amount is carried keeps main's gates, and `if A and B, ...` still demands two terminals.

The source-scope gate no longer reads the defined income term `family Virginia adjusted gross income` as a Family eligibility unit (#1842). The `family` modifier of an adjusted-gross-income aggregate is masked, and the gate reports a scope mismatch only when both main's reading and the masked reading support it, so the mask can leave a scope unresolved but never creates a mismatch. `family income`, `the family's adjusted gross income` and a stated family unit keep their reading, and every other caller of the scope reader keeps main's reading.

The completeness gate no longer demands an own-section caption's identifier as values, or reads its hyphen as a subtraction (#1827). For A.R.S. § 43-1072, "43-1072 - Earned credit for property taxes; ..." no longer demands 43 and 1072 or a formula output and companion test for the caption. The resolver now records each body-bearing row's rendered heading and its offsets in memory, without changing resolved text, hashes or attestations. The gate accepts a caption only when the first rendered heading belongs to a row in the requested section, its identifier is the section's own citation leaf, and nothing in the source points back at printed material such as "the bounds printed above". A bodyless parent's heading is never rendered, so it cannot excuse a child's opening, and the same numbers in any operative position stay demanded. A back-reference phrased outside the recognized cues is outside this heuristic.
