In US agency manuals, count only stated numeric limits as complete-source-unit
source-stated controls. `If`, `when` and `unless` sentences there are procedure,
not conditions an encoding must derive, and they no longer need paired
positive/blocking test cases either. An exception sentence that states a
numeric limit (`unless income exceeds $500`, `age 55 or older`) stays a
control. On the Oregon/Utah SNAP dispatch pilot, control demands fall from 913
to 285. Statutes, regulations and non-US sources keep exception controls; the
scope is the corpus `manual` document class, as for the US manual locators.
