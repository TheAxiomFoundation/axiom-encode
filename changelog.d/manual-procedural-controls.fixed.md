In US agency manuals, count only stated numeric limits as complete-source-unit
source-stated controls. `If`, `when` and `unless` sentences there are
procedure, not conditions an encoding must derive, and they no longer need
paired positive/blocking test cases either. An exception sentence that states a
numeric limit (`unless income exceeds $500`, `age 55 or older`, `age 16 to 18`,
`under one year of age`, `$.49 or less`) stays a control; a reference after a
comparator (`under WAC 388-450-0140`) is not a limit. On the Oregon/Utah SNAP
dispatch pilot, control demands fall from 913 to 313. Statutes, regulations and
non-US sources keep exception controls; the scope is the corpus `manual`
document class, as for the US manual locators.
