Hebrew compound numbers ("twenty and three", "a hundred and twenty", "three hundreds", "two thousand") are one number, and no constituent grounds or is owed on its own. A Hebrew fraction word is a fraction where the grammar says so — a count before it ("two fifths") or no article and a partitive after it ("a fifth of the income") — and "the fourth schedule" keeps its ordinal; half, third and quarter have nouns of their own and are always fractions. A weekday ("יום שני") and a structural reference above ten ("the eleventh chapter", "the twenty-first section") are no recall obligations. Source offsets across the cleaning pass are recovered by a walk that scores each way of passing an edit by how long the texts agree afterwards, so a space inserted every few characters is read as such; one wide edit is passed by bounded local sequence matching.
