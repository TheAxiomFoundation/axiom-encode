A walk back over a list of rates or shared-scale amounts crosses a comma only to an item that is no threshold of its own: under "בהתאמה" always, otherwise only when a Hebrew word that is no limit governor ("עד", "מעל", "בסך", "מ־" and the like) anchors the item, so "על הכנסה עד 500, 2 או 3% מס" keeps its threshold, printed or spelled, while "שיעור המס יהיה 1, 2 או 3 אחוזים" still lists three rates.
