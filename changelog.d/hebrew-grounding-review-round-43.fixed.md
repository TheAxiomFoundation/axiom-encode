A printed multiplier's scale word carries no prefix, so "בין 3 למיליון" grounds both endpoints of the range and not 3,000,000; a printed multiplier composes with a scaled tail and with descending spelled scales ("3 מיליון וחצי" is 3,500,000; "3 מיליון ומאתיים אלף" 3,200,000); a fractional or mixed multiplier reads with its scale ("חצי מיליון" is 500,000; "שלושה וחצי מיליון" 3,500,000); the construct thousand counts its multiplier ("שלושת אלפי השקלים" is 3,000); and an ordinal-shaped fraction word after an irregular feminine noun ("קרן", "עיר", "דרך") keeps both readings instead of collapsing to a duration.
