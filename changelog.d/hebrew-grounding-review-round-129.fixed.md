A feminine listed ש-noun takes its possessives on the ת construct stem ("שליחתו", "שאלתו"), so "אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3 מיליון שקלים שליחתו תקבל את המענק, והיתרה תוחזר" keeps 500; a vulgar-fraction glyph is read in every list position, the unit-bearing last member ("10, 20 ו־½ אחוז") and after a soft wrap included; and in Hebrew text a bare ASCII fraction ("1/2") and a whole with a glyph ("2½") are one number, so the ambiguity report claims only what extraction grounds.
