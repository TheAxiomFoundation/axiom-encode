Within a condition, agreement tells an adjective from the consequent's verb: an adjective agrees with the noun before it in gender and number ("אחוזים מהכנסה יומית, תחול ההוראה" reads three rates), while the consequent's verb, masculine or feminine, agrees with its own subject ("אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3 מיליון שקלים חדשים תשלם הרשות, והיתרה תוחזר" keeps 500), and a plural future verb ends in ו as no adjective does.
