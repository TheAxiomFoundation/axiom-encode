Add a pre-encode gate that blocks explicitly repealed targets. It also blocks empty or punctuation-only bodies, except omission placeholders and targets with amendment context. The gate reports nonempty heading and own-locator judgments, contents pages, existing canonical modules and unencoded explicit USC/CFR references. `encode --pre-encode-gate {report,enforce}` defaults to `enforce`; `report` makes every check advisory. Blocked runs record `status: pre_encode_blocked` with zero generation attempts. Every recorded encode outcome carries an advisory `failure_class` (`axiom-encode/failure-class/v1`) from certain signals, with `unknown` for other failures and `null` for success.
