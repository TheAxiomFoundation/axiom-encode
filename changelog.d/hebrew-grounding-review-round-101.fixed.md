A spelled three-item rate list no longer crashes the walk back; a coordinated list is headed by a plural noun of the unit's kind as the subject of the copula or construct right before it ("שיעורי המס הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים" lists three rates, commas and vavs within it joining), while a noun governing another predicate heads nothing ("שיעורי המס יחולו על הכנסה של 500 ו־2 ו־3%" keeps 500) and a conditional clause states a condition ("אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3%" keeps 500).
