A promotion dispatch can require a published review record for every unit (#2319). `axiom-encode review-record create|render|verify` writes, renders and checks `axiom-encode/candidate-review-record/v1`: the generalist review of a candidate unit with every finding, bound to the SHA-256 of the module and of the companion as reviewed. With the wrapper key `"require_review_record": true`, `targeted-signed-reencode.yml` reads the record from the candidate pull request body once and `promote-reviewed-candidate --require-review-record` signs only when every unit has a `passed` record for the exact bytes it signs. Off unless the dispatch names the key. The record is not signed: `docs/reviewed-pr-promotion.md`, "Review record", states what the gate proves and what it does not. Every run states `Review record required: yes|no` in its summary and lane pull request, and the dispatch record has `review_record_required`.
