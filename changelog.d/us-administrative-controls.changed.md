In US sources, condition sentences that only administer a program are no longer
complete-source-unit controls: a notice (`If an application is approved, the
State agency shall provide the household with written notice ...`), a
verification step, a Federal Register publication duty, an agency workflow step
(`act on the information as a reported change`, `follow the procedures in`), a
form instruction (`If married filing joint, include both names ...`), a
definitions lead-in with nothing after it, and the guidance-portal disclaimer,
superseded-history and glossary sentences that the paired-case gate already
skipped. They need neither a principal derived output nor paired
positive/blocking cases. A sentence stays a control when it states a numeric
limit or a computation, or when anything after its leading condition states
eligibility, an amount, a count, an exclusion, an allowance or deduction,
coverage, a denial, a termination or a penalty. Non-US sources are unchanged,
and US manuals keep their numeric-limit scope.
