A percent sign carries its fractional tail on plain printed and singleton spelled counts ("3% וחצי" and "שלושה% וחצי" are 3.5 percent), a hundreds remainder leaves room for a printed remainder below it ("3 אלפים ומאה ו־20" is 3,120), a negative mixed count signs the whole rate ("-3 וחצי אחוזים וחצי" is minus four percent), and an explicit range scales a thousand-plus lower endpoint as a rate ("בין 1,000 ל־2,000 אחוזים" runs from ten percent).
