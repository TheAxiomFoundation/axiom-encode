A unary sign before any spelled number signs it, percent-marked or not ("−שלושה מיליון שקלים" is minus three million, "−חצי שקל" minus half a shekel), a signed spelled upper endpoint no longer hides a shared-scale range's join ("−שלושה עד −שניים אלפים אחוזים" runs from minus thirty to minus twenty), and a shared scale word reaches every earlier alternative in a list ("1, 2 או 3 מיליון" is one, two and three million).
