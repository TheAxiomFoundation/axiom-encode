A thousand-plus printed lower bound is a complete amount whatever the upper endpoint's scale ("בין 1500 ל־3 מיליון" runs from 1,500, grouped or not), a unary sign before a spelled count the percent sign marks signs the rate ("−שלושה%" is minus three percent, "−חצי%" minus half), and signed spelled range endpoints keep their sign in value and span, the bound and the join read past the sign ("−שלושה עד שלושה אחוזים" runs from minus three, "בין −חצי ל־3 אחוזים" from minus half).
