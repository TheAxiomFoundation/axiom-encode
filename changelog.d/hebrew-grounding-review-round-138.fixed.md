Every Hebrew prefix pattern takes its separator the same way: a prefix or the article binds to its word directly, across a maqaf or across an ASCII hyphen, in the number readers, the structural references and their lists, the list headings, the measured-second guard, the money contexts, the conditional markers and the relative markers alike ("ל־חצי שנייה" is half a second and no ordinal; "התוספת ה-שנייה" is a schedule, not a two; "ה־שיעורים הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים" heads its list).
