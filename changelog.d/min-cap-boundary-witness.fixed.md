Credit a companion case at a source-stated cap in a rule that is also gated by a comparison, when the executed formula leaf is `min(amount, cap)`, the case puts the capped amount exactly at the cap and the output kinks there; such a boundary-tested cap is no longer refused as `boundary-unexercised`. A `min` inside a larger leaf is not credited by this path. The credit goes only to the cap's own source occurrence: the cap must be a parameter whose formula proof excerpts occur once in the source and state the cap value once, and the boundary must be that number. Another source threshold with the same number, a literal or computed cap, and a cap with an ambiguous excerpt are not credited by this path. A cap parameter that a formula condition also reads, and a cap number that another parameter's proof excerpt claims for its own value, are not credited either. The path also credits nothing when the source unit states the boundary's number as a boundary more than once, or when a formula condition reads a value equal to the cap's number, as a parameter, through any rule, or as a literal. The source must also state the boundary as an inclusive upper bound with no lower bound ("Up to $40"); a lower bound or a strict threshold ("more than 40 miles", "at least 40") is never credited by this path.
