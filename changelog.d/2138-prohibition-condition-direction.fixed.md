The complete-source check now takes each exception's direction from the cue that opens its own condition and the effect that condition qualifies. It skips a cross-reference that states no condition, such as a leading "Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section". It reads modal prohibitions ("must not", "may not", "shall not") and outputs that state them as negative. It reads "shall not be determined eligible" as "is not eligible" and "shall be treated as a qualified employee" as "is qualified". It sets no direction for a condition that both permits and prohibits. It also matches the plural "do not apply" wherever it matched "does not apply", and it demands `enable` of a lifted prohibition's condition (axiom-encode#2138).
