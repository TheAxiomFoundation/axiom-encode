A Hebrew percentage phrase is read whole with a joined teen or printed count and a counted fractional tail ("שנים־עשר אחוזים" is 0.12, "2 אחוזים וחצי" is 0.025, "שני אחוזים ושלושה רבעים" is 0.0275), a fraction word before a definite word is a fraction only after a copula or quantity word so an ordinal before a relative clause stays an ordinal ("דרגה חמישית המקנה"), a plural reference list ends before an amount in any unit (a comma joins non-final items only), a reference composes hundreds without a conjunction ("סעיף מאה עשרים ושלושה"), and the YAML rewrite declines a document its constructor rejects.
