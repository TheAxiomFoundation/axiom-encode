A percent marker after a mixed number makes it a rate ("3 וחצי%" and "שלושה וחצי%" are 0.035); a fraction word before "שנייה" says unit of time even under the article ("מחצית השנייה" is 0.5), the construct "שניית" is only ever the unit ("כל שניית המתנה"), and a following "אחת" says unit only where the clause before says a quantity follows ("יהיה שנייה אחת" is 1; "דירה שנייה אחת" keeps its second apartment).
