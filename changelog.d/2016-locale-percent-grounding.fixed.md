Ground rate literals against the percent wording of Belgian legal French and
Dutch and of German statutes: "pour cent", "pourcent(s)", "procent(en)",
"procentpunt(en)", "pct." and "v. H." after a figure, across a no-break
space, and after a figure restated in parentheses ("(14,75) p.c. du
montant", "pour (75) p.c."). A numeral after "pour cent" ("pour cent mille")
and "pour mille" are no percent, a figure before the parenthesis owns the
marker ("5 (2) p.c.") and keeps the parenthesis a masked note, and a figure
without a marker still never grounds N/100. A pipe-table body cell whose
marker follows its figure ("28 pour cent") types that figure only and no
longer gives its whole column rate context. Rate tables the corpus stores
flattened stay ungrounded.
