Ground rate literals against the percent wording of Belgian legal French and
Dutch and of German statutes. Belgian sources (citations under be/ and
be-<region>/, read under a new be-BE profile) read "pour cent", "pourcent(s)",
"procent(en)", "procentpunt(en)", "pct." and "v. H." after a figure, across a
no-break space, and after a figure restated in parentheses ("(14,75) p.c. du
montant", "pour (75) p.c."). German sources (de/) read "v. H." and a no-break
space before the marker. Every other source reads as on main: "28 pct." and
"28 pour cent" stay unmarked under US, UK and Canadian citations, and no
parenthesis is read as a restated figure there.

"pour cent" is a percent only where no count of a hundred can follow: before
the end of the text, ";", ")", "]", "|" or a full stop; before another rate
("5 pour cent, 10 pour cent et 15 pour cent"); or before a soft closer or a
listed phrase ("du montant", "l'an", "par an", "pour chaque enfant",
"jusqu'à la fin", "à compter du"), unless a figure, a number word,
"quelques", "plusieurs", "demi" or an opening mark comes next. So "2 pour cent
kilogrammes", "2 pour cent et quelques kilogrammes", '2 pour cent
"kilogrammes"' and "2 pour cent de deux cents kilogrammes" are no rates. Some
real rates are dropped with them ("5 pour cent applicable aux revenus", "5
pour cent de 1000 euros"); main grounds none of them either. "pour mille" is
no percent. "pct" and "v. H." match in their own case only.

In a Belgian source, a label-shaped parenthesis restates a rate only right
after a French, Dutch or German preposition or copula and before a Belgian,
Dutch or German marker or a lowercase "p.c." ("pour (75) p.c."). It stays a
masked label, as on main, before "%", an English marker or "P.C.", after any
other word ("article 5 bis (2) p.c.", "vingt-huit (28) pour cent"), and after
a figure, which owns the marker. A figure without a marker still never
grounds N/100.

Pipe tables: a Belgian or German header names a rate column only in the row
right above the delimiter row, when both have as many cells. That header row
starts the table's width, so pipe text above it no longer shifts the rate
column onto an amount. A body cell whose marker follows its figure types that
figure only, and an escaped pipe ("Type \| Category") stays inside its cell.
The width, body-cell and escaped-pipe fixes apply to every source. Rate
tables the corpus stores flattened stay ungrounded.
