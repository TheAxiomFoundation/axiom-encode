A coordinated quantity after a reference noun keeps its lower endpoint when the upper one is a compound or mixed number ("תוספת 2 עד 3 וחצי שקלים" is 2 and 3.5, "2 עד עשרים וחמישה שקלים" 2 and 25); a relational noun after an ordinal-shaped word is no unit of a fraction ("דירה חמישית בת שלושה חדרים" keeps its fifth apartment); and "שניה" is a unit of time like "שנייה" ("עשירית שניה" is 0.1).
