Within a condition a word ending in ים, ות or ת after the unit is an adjective or a noun, never a future verb, so "אם השיעורים הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים נוספים, תחול ההוראה" reads three rates; Hebrew number words never compose across a paragraph boundary ("שלושה\n\nעשר" is three, then ten) while a single line wrap still joins them; and a mixed fraction's whole number is joined to it by a space of some width only, never a Unicode paragraph, line or page separator.
