A continuation after a scaled amount is read whole before it is judged, so a separate quantity or a rate after it takes all of it ("קנס של 3 אלפים ו־100 ועשרים ימי מאסר" is a 3,000 fine and 120 days) and descending components continue until the amount ends ("3 אלפים ו־100 ועשרים ו־3" is 3,123); a shared-scale range's lower endpoint that is already a complete amount shares nothing ("בין 3 אלפים ומאה ל־4 אלפים" runs from 3,100); a range of rates reads any complete printed amount as an endpoint, mixed multipliers and remainders included ("בין 3 וחצי אלפים ל־4 אלפים אחוזים" runs from thirty-five); and a printed multiplier's tail and scale word before a percent noun are the printed pass's rate, not a phrase of their own ("4 וחצי אלפים אחוזים" is forty-five).
