A list of any length is read in time proportional to its length: the walk that carries a shared unit back over a list learns the list's heading once instead of once per member, the paragraph and currency-heading scopes are indexed once per text, trailing whitespace is trimmed in place rather than by copying the prefix, and the forward scan to a list's end runs to the list's boundary rather than 240 characters, so a conditional list of seventy rates keeps its unit and a text of thousands of clauses or members costs no more than its size.
