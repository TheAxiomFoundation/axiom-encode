Within a condition a modifier of the unit before the comma belongs to the list ("אם השיעורים הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים מההכנסה, תחול ההוראה" reads three rates; "שקלים חדשים," three amounts) while a verb running on is still the consequent; the subject phrase before a true copula runs to the copula within the clause, numbers included ("השיעורים שנקבעו בצו שר האוצר הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים"); and a signed number after a line wrap continues a headed list ("השיעורים הם 10,\n  -20 ו־30 אחוזים").
