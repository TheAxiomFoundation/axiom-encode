A list that shares a unit is read to its grammatical boundary however many members it has: the backward walks that carry a percent noun or a printed scale across "10, 11, …, 27 ו־28 אחוזים" no longer stop after a fixed number of steps, the walk back to a list's heading runs to the sentence stop rather than 160 characters, and a colon-terminated currency heading governs to the end of its paragraph rather than 240 characters, so the first members of a long list keep the unit the last one names.
