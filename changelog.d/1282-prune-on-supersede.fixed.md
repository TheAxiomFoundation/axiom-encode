Retire superseded apply manifests when `encode --apply` writes a new one. Re-generating a rule previously left its earlier manifest in place asserting a hash the file no longer had, and nothing pruned it: on rulespec-us this had put 121 attestations across 90 records out of date — one of them wrong for four weeks with CI green — because the guard found the newer record and never read the older one. A record whose every claim is now covered by the new manifest is deleted; one that also covers files the new manifest does not is kept, with only its superseded claim disclosed in the supersession ledger, so no true attestation is destroyed in order to remove a false one (axiom-encode#1282).
