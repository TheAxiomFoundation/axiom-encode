A comma before the upper endpoint of a list of rates or shared-scale amounts counts only with positive evidence of a list, an earlier item before the lower endpoint or "בהתאמה" after the unit, so a lone comma separating clauses leaves a threshold or an age what it is ("על הכנסה עד 500, 10% מס" keeps 500, "לילד שגילו 5, 3%" keeps the age) while "1, 2, 3 אחוזים" and "2, 3 מיליון שקלים, בהתאמה" still share.
