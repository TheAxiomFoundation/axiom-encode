A grouped or thousand-plus printed lower bound is a complete amount that shares no scale word ("בין 2,000 ל־3 אלפים שקלים" runs from 2,000, not two million), a range of rates reads scale from the parser's own classification so a scaled numeral such as אלפיים shares the percent unit, the percent phrase pass judges a printed multiplier by the count's own head rather than the run's first word ("2 אלפים עד שלושת אלפים ומאה אחוזים" keeps its upper endpoint), and a unary sign before a spelled or fractional count signs the rate ("−חצי אחוז" is minus half a percent).
