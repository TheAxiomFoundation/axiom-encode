Stop the `gates-collapsed` check from reading the list items after a `NOTE` paragraph as simultaneous facts of a condition inside the note. A manual list can hold a `NOTE` between two items ("The following shelter expenses are allowable:", an item, "NOTE When …, spread the fee …", more items). The note ends with a full stop and the next line is a bullet, so the note's clause ran on through the later items, and a rule that quotes the note was refused for not giving each later item its own input. For a proof excerpt that lies wholly inside the note, the check now reads the gates from the note's own paragraph, in one positively listed shape only: the chapeau wholly matches "The following [0-2 words] expenses|payments are allowable[ [0-2 words] expenses]:" or "Allow [0-2 words] expenses for the [1-3 words], including the following:"; the note is one line that starts with `NOTE`, one space and a capital letter, holds no condition cue or is the one sentence "NOTE When <clause>, <main clause>.", ends with a full stop after an open-class word and holds no word that points at later text; and the items before the note and the run of items after it each start a new capitalised item, do not start with a connector, do not dangle and do not end with a colon. Every other wording, and every other proof of the same clause, is read as before.
