Stop the `gates-collapsed` check from reading the list items after a `NOTE` paragraph as simultaneous facts of a condition inside the note. A manual list can hold a `NOTE` between two items ("The following costs are allowable:", an item, "NOTE When …, spread the fee …", more items). The note ends with a full stop and the next line is a bullet, so the note's clause ran on through the later items, and a rule that quotes the note was refused for not giving each later item its own input. The check now reads the gates of such a note from the note's own paragraph, for one positively listed shape only: the chapeau wholly matches "The following … are|is [not] allowable|allowed [as] …:" or "Allow …, including the following:"; the note is one line that starts with `NOTE`, one space and a capital letter, ends with a full stop after an open-class word and refers to nothing after it; and every item before the note and every paragraph after it to the end of the list starts a new capitalised item, does not start with a connector and does not dangle. Every other wording is read as before.
