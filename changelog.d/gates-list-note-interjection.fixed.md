Stop the `gates-collapsed` check from reading the list items after a `NOTE` paragraph as simultaneous facts of a condition inside the note. A manual list can hold a `NOTE` between two items ("The following costs are allowable:", an item, "NOTE When …, spread the fee …", more items). The note ends with a full stop and the next line is a bullet, so the note's clause ran on through the later items, and a rule that quotes the note was refused for not giving each later item its own input. The check now ends the note's clause at the note's own paragraph when, and only when, the note stands at the left margin on one line, ends with a full stop, holds no colon, semicolon or forward reference, follows at least one item (none ending with a colon) of a list whose chapeau says "following", ends with a colon and holds no condition cue or requirement word, and is followed by a new capitalised item with the same marker. Every other shape is read as before: a conditional or requirement chapeau, a note that ends with a colon or refers forward, a note with no list open before it, nested or indented items, and a plain `When` paragraph.
