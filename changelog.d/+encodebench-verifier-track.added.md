Add the EncodeBench verifier track (`benchmarks/verifier/`, runbook `docs/encodebench-verifier.md`): a benchmark of fidelity judges rather than encoders. A versioned, seeded mutator plants one of six defect kinds (amount changed, boundary flipped, conjunct dropped, and/or polarity, wrong effective date or period, wrong entity) inside known-good artifacts, always shipping the clean original as the control; a loader reads recorded real pre-fix/post-fix pairs. Judge runners share one interface (the incumbent statutory-fidelity referee on a pinned Claude model through the repo's `JudgeClient`, TypeSafe Jev through `typesafe-sdk`, and a file replay for tests), write a `results.json` bound to the suite identity, and fold into a leaderboard that reports per-kind detection AUC subject to a false-alarm ceiling, localization rate, latency, tokens and published-price cost, refusing mismatched suites, mutator versions or duplicate runner names the way `eval-board` does.
