Give the encoder of a CFR unit the RuleSpec modules of the CFR sections its source text cites. Context selection for a `us/regulation/...` unit reads `§ P.S` (title from the unit), each item of a `§§` list, and `N CFR P.S` citations with one closed grammar, drops a bare `§ P.S` that follows an `N CFR` citation of another title in the same sentence (unless `of this chapter`, `of this part` or `of this subpart` follows it), re-parses each hit with the corpus resolver, and maps it with `_source_identifier_to_relative_rulespec_path` (`regulations/42-cfr/435/811.yaml`, not the dotted `435.811.yaml`). Sections are ordered by citation count, then first citation (at most 8; the unit's own section excluded); per section at most 4 modules, cited paragraphs first, then the section file, then live subdirectory modules by depth and path; a `status: deferred` section file is kept only when no live module exists. After transitive expansion the feature adds at most 16 modules and 32,000 characters; a candidate that would exceed either bound is skipped. Companion tests of the added modules are not copied. A new prompt block lists the exports and input slot names of each cited CFR module. Prompts of other units (statutes, manuals, state regulations) are unchanged.
