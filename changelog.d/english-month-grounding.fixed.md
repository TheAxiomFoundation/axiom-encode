Numeric grounding reads an English month name used as a date (`June 1939`, `December 1, 1940`, `1 March 2024`, `January of each year`, `May 1 each year`, `the month of July`) as its month number, with the guards Hebrew month names have: the number grounds only a literal the module compares with a month-named operand (`birth_month <= 6`), never an unrelated literal of the same value, and never becomes a recall obligation. Only full names in title case or capitals count, so `may`, `march`, abbreviations and `Mayor` date nothing. The words that date a name match in any case (`JULY AND AUGUST 1939`, `IN THE MONTH OF JUNE`) and must share its line and table cell. `May` alone needs a year after it (`May 2020`, `May of each year`) or `month of` before it, so `Persons Aged 18 May Apply` dates nothing. `in`, `each`, `every`, `through`, `to`, `until` and `till` follow the verb too, so before one of them a `May` reads as the verb when its main verb can follow the date (`THE BOARD MAY IN ANY YEAR ADOPT RULES`, `The Board May in Any Year Adopt Rules`, `(2) May in each taxable year elect to apply`), and takes the date with it, at either end of a list (`PERSONS NAMED APRIL AND JUNE MAY IN ANY YEAR APPLY`). A word or a label that takes a month, a lowercase word before it and a row the date fills keep it the month (`from May through December 1990`, `Birth months: May through December 1940`, `not later than 1 May in each year`). A faithful English rendering of National Insurance Law schedule A1 part D now grounds the birth months its Hebrew original grounds.

A list or range of month names no longer dates itself, in Hebrew as in English: `יולי ואוגוסט`, `מאי עד דצמבר`, `July and August` and `June through December` need a year, a day, the year they recur in, or the word month (or the definite plural `the months`, `the calendar months of`, `החודשים`) before them. A count of months dates nothing (`within 3 months April and June`, `within one month of June`, `בתוך 3 חודשים`). Sources list people's names the same way: on main, `אנשים בשם אפריל ויוני רשאים להגיש` ("persons named April and June may apply") grounded `birth_month == 6`. In both languages, "each year" without "of" or "in" now dates a name beside a day (`May 1 each year`, `ב־1 במאי כל שנה`).

A month name, Hebrew or English, also grounds only its own month number now: a percent sign beside it no longer grounds a rate scaled from that number (on main, `החודשים יולי ודצמבר %` grounded `birth_month >= 0.12`). Month numbers now sit in a stream of their own that only the two grounding decisions read, so source completeness, proof excerpts, reanchoring and worksheet certification never see a month name as a number: since #1745 a Hebrew `יוני 1939` had become an interval bound, a boundary obligation and a counted excerpt number, and made companion tests demonstrate a formula branch for it. A day or the word month that the 40-character context window cuts in two (`1230 June`, `semimonth of May`) no longer dates a name, in either language.
