A prefixed percent noun is still a rate ("באחוז אחד" is 0.01, "באחוז וחצי" 0.015); a reference label is a whole number, so "1, 2, 4, 1,500 עד 2,500 דולר" ends its list at 4 and recalls both amounts; a range of rates shares its percent noun ("בין 2 ל־3 אחוזים" and "בין שניים לשלושה אחוזים" are 0.02 and 0.03); "מה" before a noun after an ordinal is the preposition, not the partitive, unless the clause or the noun says fraction ("לידה חמישית מהיריון נפרד" keeps its fifth birth); !!omap and !!pairs tuples go through the memoized YAML comparison; and the two Hebrew scanning tests time the pass itself, with generous whole-pipeline bounds, so a slower CI runner no longer fails them.
