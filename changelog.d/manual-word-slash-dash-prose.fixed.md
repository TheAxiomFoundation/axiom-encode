In US agency manuals, read a slash or a spaced dash between two words
(`case/individual`, `SNAP/Cash`, `Processing - SNAP`) as prose, not as division
or subtraction, so it no longer creates a complete-source-unit formula-output
obligation. On the Oregon/Utah SNAP dispatch pilot, computation demands fall
from 1,338 to 624. Statutes, regulations and non-US sources keep the generic
reading; a formula written only with words and a spaced dash now reads as
prose in a US manual.
