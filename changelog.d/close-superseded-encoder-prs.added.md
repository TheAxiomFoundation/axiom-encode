Add `scripts/close_superseded_encoder_prs.py`, a dry-run-by-default tool that closes draft signed-encoding PRs superseded by a newer open PR for the same citation and base, or by a newer signed encoding of the citation on the default branch. It keeps ready-for-review PRs, PRs with commits not authored and committed by the bot, bases of open stacked PRs, and older PRs whose RuleSpec CI passes when the newer one's does not; `--only` limits `--apply` to reviewed PR numbers.
