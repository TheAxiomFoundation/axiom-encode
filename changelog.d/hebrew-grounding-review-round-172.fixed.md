The count the numeral grammar reads counts a construct-form fraction too ("עשרים רבעי השכר" is 5) and may carry a scale word ("אלף שמיניות האחוז" is 1.25); a partitive between a fractional count and the percent noun binds them ("שלוש עשיריות של האחוז" is 0.003); a fraction word's operand, partitive and unit follow it across wrap space only, so a blank line or a paragraph separator leaves "דרגה חמישית" a fifth grade.
