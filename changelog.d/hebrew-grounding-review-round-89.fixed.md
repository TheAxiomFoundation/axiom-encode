A comma never joins a list of rates or shared-scale amounts, in the initial join or in the walk back over earlier alternatives: it separates clauses as often as it lists, and nothing in the text tells the two apart, so a threshold, an age or a condition before a comma keeps its value ("על הכנסה עד 500, 10% מס" keeps 500, "אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3% מהם" keeps 500). A list a conjunction closes shares from the joined pair on ("1 או 2 או 3 אחוזים", "1 ו־2 ו־3 מיליון" share throughout; "1, 2 או 3 אחוזים" shares the two and the three).
