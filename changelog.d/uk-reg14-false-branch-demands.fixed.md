Stop the complete-source gate from misreading three kinds of wording. The gate now reads a digit mixed percentage such as `2 6/7 per cent` as one rate with no division or computation. A rate stated as a percentage of an amount yields no separate 2, 6 or 7. In a UK corpus `falls` never counts as a condition cue, and the gate skips condition words inside a title that directly follows a reference, such as `regulation 57 (residents of a dwelling to whom regulation 14(6) does not apply)`. In every other corpus, and in text with no citation path, `falls` stays a cue. The gate no longer raises a demand for a condition it read only through those words, and #2153 tracks cues for such wordings.
