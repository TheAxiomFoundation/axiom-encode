Stop the complete-source gate from misreading three kinds of wording. The gate now reads a digit mixed percentage such as `2 6/7 per cent` as one rate with no division or computation, and finds no separate 2, 6 or 7 when the source gives that rate as a percentage of an amount. In a UK corpus the gate never counts `falls` as a condition cue and skips cues inside a title that directly follows a reference, such as `regulation 57 (residents of a dwelling to whom regulation 14(6) does not apply)`. In every other corpus, and in text with no citation path, the gate keeps `falls` as a cue. The gate no longer raises a demand for a condition it read only through `falls` or a title's words, and #2153 tracks cues for most such wordings.
