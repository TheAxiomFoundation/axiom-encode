Stop the complete-source gate from misreading three kinds of wording. The gate no longer reads the slash of a digit mixed percentage such as `2 6/7 per cent` as a division or a computation. Outside Danish and German corpora, where the numeric extractor reads that numeral as one rate, the gate also finds no separate 2, 6 or 7 inside it. In a UK corpus the gate opens no exception branch on `falls`. In every other corpus, and in text with no citation path, it keeps `falls` as a cue. In a UK corpus the gate also skips cues inside a title that directly follows a reference, such as `regulation 57 (residents of a dwelling to whom regulation 14(6) does not apply)`. The gate no longer raises a demand for a condition it read only through `falls` or a title's words, and #2153 tracks cues for most such wordings. The gate also accepts an integer Money assertion such as `1` against a computed `0.99999999999999995`, as the test runner does and as the gate already did for `1.0`.
