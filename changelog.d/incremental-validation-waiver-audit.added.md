Add opt-in `validation-waivers audit --scope incremental` with conservative dependency selection, explicit full-audit fallbacks, and selection reasons. Full scope remains the default; ledger transition checks and validation fingerprints are unchanged. Partition ownership comes from the complete sorted audit ledger, including active, pending, and removed waivers, before each shard applies its local selection. Shards using the same ledgers and partition keys collectively cover every affected waiver even when only some shards fall back to full scope. Partition reports count the complete ledger in `partition.total_paths`; `selected`, `checked`, and selection reasons describe that shard's selected paths.
