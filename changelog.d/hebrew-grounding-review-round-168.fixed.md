Only a verb of paying governs a fraction word across its recipient, and only a fraction of an amount: the recipient keeps its name and its possessor ("לעובדת בשם שירה", "לעובדת של החברה" are paid a fifth of the wage), a receiving verb's object keeps its ordinal ("קיבלה לוחית חמישית" is a fifth plate), and a recipient's own ordinal stays ("שילם לעובדת חמישית מן העובדות הזכאיות" pays a fifth employee).
