A comma joins a list of rates or shared-scale amounts, and a walk back crosses one, only to an item of a list a predicate introduces ("הם", "יהיה", "כדלקמן:", a supplement noun), reached directly or through earlier items; a threshold before a comma keeps its value whatever governs it ("על הכנסה שאינה עולה על 500, 2 או 3% מס" keeps 500, printed or spelled), and "בהתאמה" after the unit is no longer taken as evidence, since it governs the rates alone.
