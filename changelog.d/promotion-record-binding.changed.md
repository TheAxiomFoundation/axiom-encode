`promote-reviewed-candidate` now enforces the admission record's binding before transaction recovery, validation, and manifest signing: the promoted citation, canonical RuleSpec path, and primary and companion sha256 must equal the record's. The console entrypoint attaches the signing broker before CLI dispatch; these checks do not precede that attachment. Only the frozen pre-record admissions (`UNBOUND_PROMOTION_REVIEWED_RULESPEC_REFS`), which carry no binding, keep their earlier behaviour; any other record without a binding cannot be promoted.
