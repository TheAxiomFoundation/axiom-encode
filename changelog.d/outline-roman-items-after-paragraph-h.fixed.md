Read an `(i)` that opens a marker under paragraph `(h)` as a roman item when `(ii)`, not `(j)`, comes next at its level. The complete-source check no longer reports items such as 12 CFR 1006.14(h)(2)(i) to (iii) as paragraphs (i) to (iii) of the section. The rule pairs each `(i)` with a later `(ii)`, including the items of a list in running text, so a paragraph `(i)` whose own text opens a list, as in "may not (i) call; (ii) write", stays a paragraph. The rule still reads such a paragraph as a roman item in a few texts, such as one that also cites a bare `(ii)`.
