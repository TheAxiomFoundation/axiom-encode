A minus after a maqaf is a sign ("כ־−1⁄4" is -0.25, "ב־−2 אחוזים" -0.02) while a hyphen after a letter is still a prefix's; a range's lower bound takes an ASCII hyphen ("מ-2 ל-3 אחוזים" is 0.02 and 0.03); a printed whole with a spelled fractional tail is one quantity anywhere ("3 וחצי נקודות זיכוי" is 3.5, "2 ושלושה רבעים" 2.75); and "שנייה" is the unit of time, not the ordinal, after a fraction, a digit or "כל", or before "אחת" ("חצי שנייה" is 0.5; "לידה שנייה" keeps its 2).
