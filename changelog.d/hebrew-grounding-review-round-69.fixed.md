A scaled percentage reads as one rate in every form: a printed or spelled leading amount, a spelled, printed or mixed remainder, the percent noun or the sign, and a fractional tail of the rate's own or of another unit ("שלושת אלפים ו־200 אחוזים" is 3,200 percent; "3 אלפים ו־200 וחצי אחוזים" 3,200.5 percent; "שלושת אלפים% וחצי" 3,000.5 percent), with the seventy combinations covered by one regression.
