A scaled percentage keeps its fractional tail in both forms ("3 אלפים אחוזים וחצי" and "שלושת אלפים אחוזים וחצי" are 3,000.5 percent), a scaled count before the percent noun is the rate's own unless a money amount governs it ("שלושת אלפים אחוזים" is 3,000 percent; "סכום של שלושה מיליון ועשרים אחוזים" stays three million and twenty percent), and a printed multiplier's scale word before the noun is read by the printed pass, never as a count of one.
