Targeted signed re-encodes that open a PR now stop, before the signing approval and any model call, when the target RuleSpec repository's pinned encoder cannot admit the dispatch encoder. A pin older than #1859 admits only its exact commit; a pin that contains #1859 but not #1883 admits nothing in CI; a pin that contains #1883 admits its exact commit or a first-parent `main` descendant. Artifact-only runs are reported. `ENCODER_PIN_ADMISSION_OVERRIDE=true` reports without blocking. The SNAP dispatch queue does not count such a refusal as an attempt and holds dispatch on that RuleSpec tip.
