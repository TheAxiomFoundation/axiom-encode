A percent marker after a fraction word is the partitive the percent word is ("חמישית%" is 0.002, "רבע%" 0.0025); a range of rates shares its percent marker as it shares the noun ("בין 2 וחצי ל־3 וחצי%" is 0.025 and 0.035, "2 עד 3%" 0.02 and 0.03); and a prefixed fraction word or a temporal preposition before "שנייה" says unit of time ("לחצי שנייה", "למחצית השנייה" are 0.5; "לאחר שנייה אחת" is 1) while "דירה שנייה אחת" keeps its second apartment.
