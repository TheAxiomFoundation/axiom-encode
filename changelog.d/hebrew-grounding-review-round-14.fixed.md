A Hebrew percentage phrase is read whole with its spelled count and fractional tail ("אחוז וחצי" is 0.015, "שני אחוזים וחצי" is 0.025), a singular structural reference ends at a comma so a following amount stays a recall obligation, a fraction word before a definite noun or after a copula is a fraction ("חמישית ההכנסה", "יהיה חמישית משכרו"), and a structural reference accepts a lone cardinal and a teen after hundreds ("סעיף שלוש", "סעיף מאה ואחד עשר").
