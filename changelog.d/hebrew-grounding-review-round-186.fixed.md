A bidirectional mark inside a numeric token is nothing to the cleaner and the direct percentage reader ("−‏.5%" and "−.5‏%" are −0.005); a fraction before a scaled upper endpoint, counted or not, takes the scale through the shared-scale pass ("בין חצי לבין 3 אלפים אחוזים" runs from 5); and a comma-grouped number with a decimal part is read whole ("1,234.5%" is 12.345), a comma-grouped whole in Hebrew text being grouped thousands.
