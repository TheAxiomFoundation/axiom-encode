A rate word's subject phrase keeps its relative clauses and construct complements ("הריבית שהבנק גובה היא 10 או 30%", "הריבית על הלוואת עובד תהיה 10 או 30%" are two rates), a definite amount noun that no phrase holds is the subject of the predicate after it ("אז הקנס יהיה 50 או 2%" is fifty shekels), and every rate word in a clause is a candidate, so a later "בשיעור של" governs its pair when an earlier rate's expression has ended.
