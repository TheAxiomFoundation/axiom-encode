A vav-bound count after a percentage is a new quantity ("שלושה אחוזים וחמישה שקלים" is 0.03 and 5); a fractional tail that carries its own unit is not the rate's ("שני אחוזים וחצי שקל" is 0.02 and 0.5); a printed whole with a spelled fractional tail is one count ("3 וחצי אחוזים" is 0.035); a hyphen after a Hebrew prefix is a separator, not a minus ("בין 2 ל-3 אחוזים" is 0.02 and 0.03, "-3 אחוזים" still -0.03); and the unit lexicon takes disability, work-status and institution nouns and derives every masculine plural's construct form ("100 מקבלי קצבאות", "100 עובדי המפעל" stay quantities).
