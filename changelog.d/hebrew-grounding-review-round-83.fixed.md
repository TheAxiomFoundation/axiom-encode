A comma join in a list of rates or shared-scale amounts applies only where a Hebrew word anchors the list, so English currency and age operands before a comma stay what they are ("For income up to $500, 10% applies" keeps 500; "under age 5, 3%" keeps the age), and tens no longer absorb a count that opens counted hundreds or a well-formed teen of its own ("שלושים וארבע מאות" is thirty and four hundred, "עשרים ושלושה עשר" twenty and thirteen), while "מאה ואחד עשר" stays 111 and a malformed teen stays no teen.
