CI runs the test suite as eight parallel shards (`tests/ci_shards.py`), so the `test (3.13)` check takes 30 to 40 minutes, not 2 hours 52 minutes. That check now passes only when every shard ran and passed its tests and their reports show that together they ran the whole suite. Without `AXIOM_ENCODE_TEST_SHARD`, `pytest` runs every test as before.
