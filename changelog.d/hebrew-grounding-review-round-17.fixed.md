A standalone carriage return ends a line ("CHAPTER 1\rThe benefit is 100 dollars." keeps its 100 under every profile) while a CRLF keeps its width; a definite ordinal before the percent noun stays an ordinal ("הילד השני אחוז וחצי" is the second child and 0.015); a fractional count is read ("חצי אחוז ורבע" is 0.0075, "שלושה רבעים אחוז" 0.0075); a fraction of a named amount is a fraction after any verb ("ישלם חמישית משכרו" is 0.2) and the negative ordinal cases hold; a comma list ends before a coordinated amount ("1, 2, 4, 100 או 200 דולר" recalls 100 and 200); a range is one list item ("1 עד 3 ו־5"); a spelled reference reads the whole numeral grammar ("סעיף שני אלפים", "פרק שנים עשר אלף"); "שביעית" and "תשיעית" are mixed-number tails; and the percentage pass tokenizes the text once (4,000 phrases in 0.07 s, not 3.8 s).
