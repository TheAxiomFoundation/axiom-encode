An ordinal-shaped fraction word before a time unit and a temporal phrase is read by evidence, not by word endings: a listed noun before it says ordinal, a duration verb (with a conjunction prefix or not) says duration, and otherwise the unit decides, a fraction of a second, minute or hour being a duration ("פעלה עשירית שנייה לאחר") and a fifth something a year or month after being an ordinal ("מרפאה חמישית שנה לאחר"); and a citation word's internal whitespace is free ("על  פי תוספת 5" keeps schedule 5).
