Stop the encode repair loop when every residual blocking issue of a validator-rejected attempt is a certain non-encoder blocker: a RuleSpec checkout root the rules engine refuses (`migration/schema`), an empty authoritative corpus body (`corpus/content`), or a citation or target path that differs from the signed dispatch contract (`process`). No further model call and no escalation follow; the outcome records `retry_stopped` (`axiom-encode/retry-stopped/v1`) and its `failure_class` takes that class. Any other issue keeps the retry. `failure_classes.classify_issue` classifies one issue from these certain signals only.
