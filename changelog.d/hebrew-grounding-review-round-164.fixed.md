A schedule reference stays a reference before a verb or the statute's construct form ("התוספת השנייה מגדירה תשלום", "של פקודת מס הכנסה"), since only a fraction-shaped ordinal before a partitive is a fraction; the future "תהווה" is a predicate like "מהווה"; and "מן" after an ordinal a feminine noun carries is a whole's partitive only where it names an amount ("פנייה חמישית מן הציבור" is a fifth request).
