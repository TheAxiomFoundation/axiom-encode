A unit abbreviation is matched with gershayim as well as an ASCII quote ("50 ק״ג" and "100 עד 200 מ״ר" after a reference list stay quantities); the construct cardinal "שני"/"שתי" composes inside a compound ("עשרים ושני הימים" is 22, "מאה ושני הימים" 102) while staying no cardinal on its own or under the article; and a spelled fraction is a range endpoint that shares the percent noun ("בין חצי לשלושה אחוזים" is 0.005 and 0.03, "רבע עד חצי אחוז" 0.0025 and 0.005).
