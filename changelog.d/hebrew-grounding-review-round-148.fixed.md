A rate word reaches the pair it governs and no further: across the modifiers of its own expression, never across a new predicate ("יוטל") or an amount noun that governs the pair itself ("קנס של 50 או 2%" is fifty shekels and two percent), and across a line wrap but not a blank line; a money noun reaches its amount across any spacing or wrap in its sentence ("הקנס … יהיה 3 מיליון ו־30 ימי מאסר" is a fine and a term).
