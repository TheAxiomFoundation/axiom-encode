An "או" alternative that a singular money noun governs directly is one amount beside the other ("קנס של 50 או 2% מהמחזור" keeps 50, "סכום של 500 או 3 מיליון" keeps 500), a rate word before the alternatives makes them a list of rates however large ("בשיעור של 125 או 150 אחוזים" is 125 and 150 percent), and the hundred-plus rule of the previous round, which misread that overtime form, is gone.
