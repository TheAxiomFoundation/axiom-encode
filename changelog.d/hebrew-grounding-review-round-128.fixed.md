A listed ש-noun inflects from its base ("שם" gives "שמנו", both marker and noun, so the list is reported ambiguous) while an entry that is itself an inflection stays fixed ("שממנו" remains the preposition); fraction words and glyphs are list body and endpoints ("השיעורים הם שני שלישים, 10 ו־30 אחוזים" shares the unit; "½" is a printed endpoint); a mixed ASCII fraction in Hebrew text is one number ("2 1/2" is 2.5); and an English percent word after a fraction slash scales it ("10 1⁄4 percent" is 0.1025).
