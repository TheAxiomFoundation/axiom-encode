A fractional tail after a percent unit that carries a unit of its own stays that unit's ("3 אלפים אחוזים וחצי שקל" is 30 and half a shekel), and a scaled percentage with a remainder is one rate outside a money context ("3 אלפים וחמש מאות אחוזים", "שלושת אלפים וחמש מאות%" and "3 אלפים ו־200%" are 3,500 and 3,200 percent), while under a money amount the remainder before the percent unit stays a rate of its own.
