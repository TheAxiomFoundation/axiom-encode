A rate word's reach is read as a phrase: its subject with attributives, relative clauses and prepositional phrases ("הריבית על ההלוואה", "הריבית השנתית החלה על יתרת ההלוואה הכוללת"), its predicate whether a future verb, a copula, a participle or a bare predicative ("תהיה", "היא", "עומדת", "גבוהה"), and the predicate's connectors ("על", "לפחות", "בשיעור של"); a new subject ("אז הקנס יהיה 50 או 2%") or a second predicate takes the pair away.
