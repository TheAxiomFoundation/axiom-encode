A range of rates keeps a negative lower endpoint ("-2 עד 3 אחוזים" and "בין -2 ל־3 אחוזים" are -0.02 and 0.03) and reads a fractional one whole ("1/2 עד 3 אחוזים" is 0.005 and 0.03, a number after a slash never being an endpoint of its own); and the unit lexicon that keeps a quantity out of a reference list now covers measures, weights, volumes, energy and counts of people and things, so "סעיפים 1, 2, 4, 100 עד 200 מטרים" recalls both amounts.
