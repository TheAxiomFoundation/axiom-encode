A fraction that a percent sign follows (`16 1⁄2%`, `1⁄4%`) is one rate to ground and recall, and the plain percentage matcher no longer takes its denominator as a second one. A fraction-slash run needs a whole numerator (`.5⁄2` and `-.5⁄2` produce no fraction from a decimal's substring). A Hebrew structural reference — a chapter, schedule, part, sign, section, paragraph, table, column, item or regulation named by an ordinal or a number — is no recall obligation, while a substantive ordinal ("the fourth child") still is. Source offsets are recovered by a bounded resynchronising walk across the cleaning pass's local edits, linear on repetitive text, with sequence matching (autojunk on) only for an edit wider than the walk's window.
