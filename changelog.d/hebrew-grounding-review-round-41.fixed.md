An ordinal-shaped fraction word before a time unit and a temporal phrase that nothing in the text settles ("מרפאה חמישית שנה לאחר", "נעדר חמישית שנה לאחר") is recorded with both readings, the ordinal and the fraction, so either grounds and the recall obligation is met by either; and the numeral grammar composes millions and billions with construct counts and scaled fractional tails ("שלושה מיליון" is 3,000,000, "מיליון וחצי" 1,500,000, "אלף וחצי" 1,500).
