A range endpoint is classified before a bare endpoint shares the fraction word of the counted fraction after it: a fraction of its own ("בין חצי לשלושה רבעים האחוז" runs from 0.005) shares nothing, a mixed number's tail ("בין 2 ל־3 ושלושה רבעים אחוזים" runs from 0.02) is no unit, a partitive after the counted fraction ("שלוש עשיריות של האחוז") hides no unit, and a mixed count of a fraction word ("2 וחצי עשיריות") is complete.
