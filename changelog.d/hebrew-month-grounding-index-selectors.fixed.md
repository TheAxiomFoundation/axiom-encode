Numeric grounding reads a Hebrew Gregorian month name used as a date (ינואר…דצמבר, מרס/מרץ) as its month number, and that number grounds only a literal the module compares with a month-named operand (`birth_month <= 6`), never an unrelated literal of the same value. A group made only of the homographs מאי and מרץ is dated only by a year after it or "בחודש" (in the month of) before it; a homograph listed with another month name ("מאי עד דצמבר", "מאי ויוני") is dated with that list. A private integer rule that only picks a row of the local tables indexed by it, and that every other local formula reads only as that table's subscript, counts as a structural selector whatever its name, so its row labels are neither ungrounded nor lifted into parameters by the embedded-scalar repair.
