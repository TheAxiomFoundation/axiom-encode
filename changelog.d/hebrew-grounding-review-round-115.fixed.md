Within a condition what follows a verb-shaped word tells the consequent's verb: a plural one ends in ו and a singular one is followed by its definite subject ("אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3 מיליון שקלים לעובד ישלם המעסיק, והיתרה תוחזר" keeps 500), which no adjective or construct complement is ("אחוזים מדמי אבטלה, תחול ההוראה" reads three rates); and a lexical ש-word such as "שכיר" opens no relative clause.
