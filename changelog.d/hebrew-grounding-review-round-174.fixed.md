The counted-fraction vocabulary carries every plural the fraction table does ("שלוש רביעיות" is 0.75, "שתי שלישיות" two thirds); a printed whole with a spelled tail counts a fraction word ("3 וחצי עשיריות האחוז" is 0.0035, "ל־3 וחצי עשיריות מהשכר" 0.35) and is not read again on its own; the percent sign after a printed figure binds across wrap space only, so "3 וחצי" before a blank line and "%" is three and a half.
