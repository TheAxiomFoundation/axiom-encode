A source unit that prints no date no longer takes its start date from a neighbouring module (#2212). The encoder prompt now states the start-date rule for a date-silent unit: a printed date, the corpus `expression_date` or `source_as_of`, the evidence floor (first day of the first full calendar month on or after `source_as_of`; an evidence-coverage floor, not a claimed legal commencement date) or the `0001-01-01` scaffold. The new complete-source check `complete-source-unit:effective-date/start-unsupported` refuses the narrow case: a manual unit without a printed date, imports or amendment context whose `effective_from` is earlier than `source_as_of` and has no other support. It runs only in complete-source mode (new encodes and promotions), so `validate` on existing modules is unchanged.
