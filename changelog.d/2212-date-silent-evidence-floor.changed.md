For a corpus manual unit without amendment context, the encoder prompt now states a start-date rule (#2212). In complete-source mode, the new `complete-source-unit:effective-date/start-unsupported` check refuses unsupported starts when its detector finds no date or year in the source text. It accepts the `0001-01-01` scaffold, readable corpus `expression_date` and `source_as_of` values, the evidence floor, dates from proof-atom excerpts, and version starts of exports that the module verifiably imports. The proposed evidence-floor convention selects the first day of the first full calendar month on or after `source_as_of`; it establishes no legal commencement date or corpus custody history. Maintainers have not settled the six methodology choices.

For start selection, a nonempty `versions` list overrides rule-level fields. The check reads `effective_from` or its `from` alias from those versions, or from the rule when `versions` is absent, empty, blank or comment-only. It refuses both aliases together, supplies no default for a missing start, and leaves explicit `null` or `~` version containers for the compiler to refuse. This start projection ignores YAML `<<` inheritance, as the engine does. Other document classes retain their prompts. Validation without complete-source mode does not run this check on existing modules.
