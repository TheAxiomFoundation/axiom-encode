The whole number of a mixed fraction stands on the fraction's own line ("10\n\n1⁄4" is ten, then a quarter); within a condition a future verb or a plural participle after the unit is the clause running on while an adjective, a construct noun or a relative clause modifies the unit ("אם השיעורים הם 10, 20 ו־30 אחוזים מהכנסה חייבת, תחול ההוראה" reads three rates; "שקלים משולמים כמענק" heads nothing); a colon inside a subject parenthetical is no clause stop ("בצו (להלן: הצו) הם"); and a no-break or em space indents a wrapped list like an ASCII space.
