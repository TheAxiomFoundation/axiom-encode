A spaced dash is no range join, so a tax schedule row ("על כל שקל חדש מ־84,120 – 10%") keeps its threshold and its rate; a number with a unit after it is a quantity whatever noun precedes it ("תוספת 2 שקלים", "תוספת שתי נקודות זיכוי" are 2) while a schedule reference stays structural; a prefix hyphen before a symbol percentage is no sign ("מ-2 ל-3%" is 0.02 and 0.03, "-3%" still -0.03); a duration limit before "שנייה" says unit of time ("לא יעלה על שנייה אחת" is 1); and the feminine third and quarter ("שלישית", "רביעית") are range endpoints and mixed-number tails like their masculine forms.
