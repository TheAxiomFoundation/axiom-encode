An ordinal after any feminine noun stays an ordinal before a definite noun that begins the next phrase ("בבדיקה חמישית השכר נמצא תקין", "בעיר חמישית השכר גבוה" are a fifth inspection and a fifth city), a balance and a benefit are amount nouns ("חמישית מיתרת החשבון", "חמישית מההטבות" are fifths), and a supplement of a fraction of an amount ("תוספת חמישית מהשכר") is an amount to encode, not a schedule reference.
