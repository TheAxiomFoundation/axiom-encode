Read count thresholds at the exception-pair test check by the condition's number, not by its wording or the input name (axiom-encode#2305). For a condition that the shared interval parser leaves unread, the pair check now reads "N or more", "N or fewer" and the other postfix comparisons as an inclusive one-sided interval, and "fewer than N", "no fewer than N" and "not fewer than N" as their "less than" and "at least" forms, so a `dtype: Count` input with cases at N and next to N passes for every wording that creates a pair obligation; a `dtype: Boolean` input that restates such a comparison is now refused, as it already was for "at least N". A numeric case pair is also linked to its condition without the input-name test when the condition states one limit, the source unit states that number once, the output's formula compares the declared local input directly with that number, the input's dtype fits the number, one case sits exactly on the number, and the check reads from the source whether the condition enables or blocks the output. The shared interval parser, the boundary check and every other reader are unchanged, and a condition whose comparison stands behind a distant negation ("has not paid rent for more than one consecutive month") stays unread.
