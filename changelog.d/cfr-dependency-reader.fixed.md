Read CFR dependencies in complete-source deferral reasons with one closed grammar (`42 CFR 435.811`, `42 C.F.R. § 435.811`, `§ 435.811` with the unit's own title, `us/regulation/42/435/811`, each with optional paragraph fragments), re-checked by the corpus resolver's citation grammar. In a CFR section unit such a dependency binds only to a leaf branch whose own text cites the same title, part and section (and the same fragments when both sides give them) with link syntax, cites no other provision, and links it in every sentence. The generic reader no longer keys `§`, `us/regulation/` or dotted CFR citations by their first number in CFR units, so a part-only `§ 435` or `section 435` reason no longer binds to every `§ 435.x` in the source.
