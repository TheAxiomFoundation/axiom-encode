A comma joins a list of rates or shared-scale amounts only when "בהתאמה" follows the unit ("1, 2, 3 אחוזים, בהתאמה"); an earlier range or alternative before the comma is no evidence, so "על הכנסה של 100 עד 500, 10% מס" keeps its thresholds and "לילד שגילו 4 או 5, 3%" its ages, a spelled operand before the comma is judged as a printed one is ("עד חמש מאות, 3 מיליון" keeps 500), and a label with a possessive suffix ("שגילו 5") stops the walk back as the bare label does.
