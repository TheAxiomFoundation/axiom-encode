Ground numbers that Chinese statutes write in characters. Taiwan's Statute for Industrial Innovation Article 10-1 states its spend band as 新臺幣一百萬元以上、二十億元以下, its rates as 百分之五, 百分之三 and 百分之三十, and its window from 中華民國一百十四年一月一日, with no ASCII digit, so the CI numeric-grounding gate rejected every rate and threshold of a correct encoding. Character numerals (一百十四, 二十億, 一億二千萬) now map to their values; 百分之N is read as the rate N/100 and, with an amount followed by 元, enters the source inventory; other numeral runs (三年, 第一項) and a 民國 (Minguo) era year's Gregorian reading (114 + 1911 = 2025) only ground, so a numeral used as a word or a label is never a value the encoding must recall.
