A suffix moves a listed ש-noun's final letter to its medial form, so "שכנו ישלם" and "שחקניו ישלמו" hide no verb ("אם התשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3 מיליון שקלים שכנו ישלם, והיתרה תוחזר" keeps 500), while the unmistakable relative markers ("שממנו", "שעליה", the ועדה family) are read before the lexical nouns; and the traditional units "חמשה" and "שלש" read as five and three, alone, in teens, in percentages and before a scale word.
