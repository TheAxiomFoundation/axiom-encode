A list wrapped onto an indented line keeps its shared unit ("10,\n            20 ו־30 אחוזים"): a bounded backward search measures its window before the whitespace that ends at its position, and a blank line in that whitespace stays a boundary; and a list inside a condition is decided by the boundary that decides it however long the clause between ("אחוזים מההכנסה החייבת אשר שולמה … הבקשה, תחול ההוראה" closes it), never by a count of words.
