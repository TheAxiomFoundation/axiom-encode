A vav never joins a list of rates or shared-scale amounts, in the initial pair or in the walk back: the vav-paired list does not occur in the statute text these passes serve, and the same vav joins clauses ("ההכנסה עומדת על 500 ו־2 ו־3% ממנה ינוכו" keeps 500), so the "בהתאמה", copula and conjunction-kind rules of rounds 92 to 95 are gone with it; an "או" alternative that a comparison follows ("500 או 2% מהמחזור, לפי הגבוה") or that opens with a bare hundred-plus number shares no percent unit, while "או" lists and bounded ranges share as before.
