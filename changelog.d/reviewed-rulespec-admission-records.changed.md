Reviewed RuleSpec head admissions are now one code-owner-reviewed record per country and commit under `data/reviewed-rulespec-refs/<country>/<sha>.json` (schema `axiom-encode/reviewed-rulespec-admission/v1`), loaded by a strict loader. `REVIEWED_RULESPEC_REFS` and `REVIEWED_RULESPEC_PR_BASES` are derived from the records; every pre-record entry migrated unchanged, with no promotion binding. Supervisor provisioning exports the records from the attested encoder commit to `/opt/axiom-verification/reviewed-rulespec-refs`, and `promote-reviewed-candidate` now requires `--reviewed-rulespec-refs-root`.
