A percent phrase whose count opens a scaled amount's continuation carrying a scale word of its own is left whole to the continuation reader ("סכום של 3 מיליון ושני אלפים וחמש מאות אחוזים" is three million and 25 percent, not 2,000 and five), and a range of rates parses a spelled lower endpoint before judging its scale, so a remainder after the scale word no longer hides it ("בין שלושת אלפים ומאה ל־4 אלפים אחוזים" runs from 31 percent).
