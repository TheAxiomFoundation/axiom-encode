Feminine and further count nouns mark a quantity after a reference list ("100 עובדות", "50 מבוטחות", "30 נשים", "12 משפחות" stay substantive); a range of rates reads a printed lower endpoint before a ל-prefixed spelled upper one ("בין 2 לשלושה אחוזים" is 0.02 and 0.03) and takes an attached מ prefix as the bound ("משניים לשלושה אחוזים", "מחצי לשלושה אחוזים"); and a construct two before a teen ten ("מאה ושני עשר") is rejected as a number instead of raising, so imperfect source text never crashes extraction.
