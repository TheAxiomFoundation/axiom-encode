The prefix stack the numeric text view binds across a maqaf or a hyphen is the grammar's own: the conjunction, then ש or כש, then a preposition or the article ("וכש־המתינה", "כשה־שיעורים", "ומה־שכר"), so every stack reads as its attached form; a plural heading noun carries the same stack, and a "כש" in it opens the condition ("כשהתשלומים הם 500, 2 או 3 מיליון שקלים ישולמו" splits at the consequent as "כאשר התשלומים" does).
