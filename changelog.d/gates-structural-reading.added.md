Add a structural reading of bullet lists for the `gates-collapsed` check, behind a switch that is off (`source_list_structure.STRUCTURAL_GATE_READING_ENABLED`). With the switch off the validator does not change. With the switch on, the check reads the unit text as blocks and lists. A sentence inside a list of alternatives ("when|if any of the following ...:" or "Any of the following ...:") no longer runs on into the next items, and its own fact still needs a positive input. A list of alternatives that ends the unit, under a plain affirmative chapeau and with plain items, is verified on the value of the formula: each alternative needs inputs of its own that name every word of the item and grant the rule whatever the other alternatives are. A rule that quotes two items or more of a list under a closed conjunctive chapeau is read against the whole list. Every other case keeps the flat reading, finding for finding. See `docs/gates-structural-reading.md`.
