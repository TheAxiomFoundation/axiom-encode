Add a structural reading of bullet lists for the `gates-collapsed` check, behind a switch that is off (`source_list_structure.STRUCTURAL_GATE_READING_ENABLED`). With the switch off the validator does not change. With the switch on, the check reads the unit text as blocks and lists: a list whose chapeau has one of two closed forms ("when|if any of the following ...:" or "Any of the following ...:") is a list of alternatives; a sentence inside such a list no longer runs on into the next items; the clause of the chapeau ends with the list; the formula is evaluated so that each alternative needs inputs of its own, combined with `or`; and a rule that quotes only items of a list is read against the whole list. Every other list keeps the flat reading. See `docs/gates-structural-reading.md`.
