The coordinated-quantity guards are deterministic (every token has one role, so a run of vav-bound number words no longer backtracks exponentially); a spelled comma list of amounts after a singular noun keeps every amount ("תוספת שתיים, שלוש או ארבע נקודות זיכוי"); a percentage is shared across every alternative ("1 או 2 או 3 אחוזים" and "1, 2 או 3 אחוזים" are 0.01, 0.02 and 0.03); and a temporal phrase after a unit keeps an ordinal only where no clause context says a quantity follows ("עשירית שקל לאחר הגשת הבקשה" is 0.1).
