How much you repay depends on your income: the amount you earn, including bonuses and overtime, before tax and other deductions. You repay a percentage of your income over the income threshold for your type of loan. The yearly income thresholds are: Plan 1, £26,900; Plan 2, £29,385; Plan 4, £33,795; Plan 5, £25,000; Postgraduate Loan, £21,000. You repay 9% of your income over the threshold if you are on Plan 1, Plan 2, Plan 4, or Plan 5. You repay 6% of your income over the threshold if you are on a Postgraduate Loan plan. If you are self-employed or complete a Self Assessment tax return for another reason, HMRC will work out how much you repay each year from your Self Assessment tax return. Your repayments will be based on your income for the whole year.
