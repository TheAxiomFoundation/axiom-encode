--- Source: uk/guidance/govuk/disability-living-allowance-children ---
Disability Living Allowance (DLA) for children may help with the extra costs of looking after a child who is under 16. The DLA rate is between £30.30 and £194.60 a week.

--- Source: uk/guidance/govuk/disability-living-allowance-children/rates ---
Care component weekly rates: lowest £30.30 for help for some of the day; middle £76.70 for frequent help or continual supervision during the day, supervision at night; highest £114.60 for help or supervision throughout both day and night. Mobility component weekly rates: lower £30.30 where the child is 5 years or over; higher £80.00 where the child is 3 years or over.
