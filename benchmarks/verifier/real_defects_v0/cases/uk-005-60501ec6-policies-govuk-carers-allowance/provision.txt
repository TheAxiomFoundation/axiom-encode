You could get £86.45 a week if you care for someone at least 35 hours a week and they get certain benefits. You need to spend at least 35 hours a week caring for someone. All of the following must apply: You're 16 or over. You're not in full-time education. You're not studying for 21 hours a week or more. Your earnings are £204 or less a week after tax, National Insurance and expenses. If you live in Scotland, you need to apply for Carer Support Payment instead of Carer's Allowance.
