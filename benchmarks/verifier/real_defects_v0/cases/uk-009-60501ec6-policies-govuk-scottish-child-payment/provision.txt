--- Source: uk/guidance/mygovscot/scottish-child-payment ---
Scottish Child Payment is a weekly payment of £28.20 that you can get for every child you look after who's under 16 years of age. You'll get the payment every 4 weeks if your application is successful. Only one person can get Scottish Child Payment for a child.

--- Source: uk/guidance/mygovscot/scottish-child-payment/who-should-apply ---
You can apply whether you're in work or not, as long as you live in Scotland, you or your partner are the main person looking after a child who's under 16 years old, and you or your partner are getting one of the qualifying benefits, such as Universal Credit. Child Benefit on its own is not an accepted benefit.
