(Effective 1/1/2026) Effective 1/1/2026 59-10-104. Tax basis -- Tax rate -- Exemption. (1) A tax is imposed on the state taxable income of a resident individual as provided in this section. (2) For purposes of Subsection (1) , for a taxable year, the tax is an amount equal to the product of: (a) the resident individual's state taxable income for that taxable year; and (b) 4.45%. (3) This section does not apply to a resident individual exempt from taxation under Section 59-10-104.1 .
